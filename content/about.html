Project

Plain Ubuntu for Rockchip single-board computers, on Armbian’s kernels and bootloaders.

Roxora’s kernels, device trees and bootloaders come from Armbian. If Roxora is useful to you, consider supporting Armbian: armbian.com/donate.

Roxora is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Canonical, the Armbian project or Rockchip.

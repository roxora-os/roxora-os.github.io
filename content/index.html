What Roxora is

Roxora builds images for Rockchip boards that pair Armbian’s kernel and bootloader with a stock Ubuntu userspace. Armbian does the hard part: kernels, U-Boot and board support for a long list of Rockchip boards. Its images also put Armbian’s own userland on top, with its first-run wizard, config tool and defaults. Roxora keeps Armbian’s hardware packages and replaces the rest with what you would get from Ubuntu itself, so userspace updates come straight from the Ubuntu archive.
